# Privacy Policy for ThinkGraph

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

## 1. Overview
ThinkGraph is a web-based interactive visual modeling and diagramming tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
ThinkGraph operates primarily as a client-side application running directly within your web browser. We do not operate external database servers that log, store, or profile your private diagrams or usage data. Diagrams and preferences are stored locally on your device via browser LocalStorage.

## 3. Google OAuth & Google Drive Integration
ThinkGraph offers an optional feature allowing users to sign in with their Google account to save and open diagrams directly to/from their personal Google Drive storage.

- **Scopes Requested:** `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata`, `openid`, `email`, `profile`.
- **File Access:** ThinkGraph ONLY accesses files that were specifically created or opened by ThinkGraph. ThinkGraph cannot and does not read, modify, or delete any of your other personal files or folders in Google Drive.
- **Tokens:** Temporary Google access tokens are kept solely in your browser's temporary session storage. These tokens are never transmitted to or stored on any third-party server.

### Google API Limited Use Disclosure
ThinkGraph's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal data or diagram contents with third parties.
