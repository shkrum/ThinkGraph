Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

ThinkGraph ("we", "our", or "the application") is a web-based interactive visual modeling and diagramming tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when using ThinkGraph.

2. Data Collection and Usage

ThinkGraph operates primarily as a client-side web application in your browser. We do not collect, harvest, or sell personal information.

  • Local Storage: Diagrams you create, user interface preferences, and configuration settings are saved locally in your browser's LocalStorage and SessionStorage.
  • No Server-Side Tracking: We do not operate external database servers that log, store, or profile your private diagrams or usage data.

3. Google OAuth & Google Drive Integration

ThinkGraph offers an optional feature allowing users to sign in with their Google account to save and open diagrams directly to/from their personal Google Drive storage.

  • Requested Scopes: ThinkGraph requests limited permissions (including https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, openid, email, and profile).
  • File Access: ThinkGraph ONLY accesses files that were specifically created or opened by ThinkGraph itself. ThinkGraph cannot and does not read, modify, or delete any of your other personal files or folders in Google Drive.
  • Authentication Tokens: Temporary Google access tokens are kept solely in your browser's temporary session storage to communicate directly with Google's APIs. These tokens are never transmitted to any third-party server.
Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
ThinkGraph's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal data or diagram files with any third parties or advertisers.

5. Security

Communication between your browser and Google Drive occurs directly through secure encrypted HTTPS connections managed by Google's official identity and API endpoints.
